I study how robots learn to interact with the physical world.

I am a PhD student interested in robot learning, robotic manipulation, and dexterous manipulation. My research focuses on enabling robots to acquire robust manipulation skills from demonstrations, simulation, and embodied interaction.

I am especially interested in reusable manipulation skills that generalize across objects, scenes, and robotic platforms.

Research

Directions & interests

  • Dexterous manipulation

    Learning-based manipulation with robotic hands, with an emphasis on perception, contact-rich interaction, and policy learning.

  • Learning from demonstrations

    Transferring human demonstrations and motion references into executable robot behaviors through imitation and visuomotor learning.

  • Simulation to the real world

    Training policies in simulation and deploying them on real robotic systems, using reinforcement learning and sim-to-real methods.
